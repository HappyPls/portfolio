About

I'm a game artist focused on character sculpting, environment props, and hardsurface modeling for real-time engines. I work in Maya, Blender, ZBrush, and Substance 3D Painter, and render my final pieces in Marmoset Toolbag. I also design game UI: on Onslaught, an itch.io project at KPU, I was Project Manager and UI/UX & Generalist Artist. I'm looking for my first full-time role in the industry and I'm eager to learn from a team while contributing wherever my skills fit best.
